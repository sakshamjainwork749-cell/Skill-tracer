# SkillTrace demo walkthrough

Use this sequence to demonstrate the complete closed loop. Seed identities and organizations are synthetic.

## 1. Public entry — 30 seconds

1. Open `/`.
2. Move through the four journey stages.
3. Select the language control to switch key copy between English, मराठी, and हिंदी.
4. Open the Trainee role card.

Explain that SkillTrace begins where conventional training portals usually stop: after certification.

## 2. Trainee passport — 45 seconds

1. On `/trainee`, locate the evidence/confidence chip and current Outcome Passport.
2. Point out course hours, certification, livelihood state, starting/current wage, retention markers, and skill relevance.
3. Explain that the three retention markers have different evidence dates.
4. Select **Update outcome**.

## 3. Low-burden update — 45 seconds

1. Choose **Employed**.
2. Enter a role, company, start date, wage band, and work location.
3. Optionally drag a fictional appointment letter into the proof slot.
4. Submit and show the success state.
5. Note that the next 3/6/12-month follow-up is scheduled and the event is audited.

Repeat a branch briefly by previewing **Self-employed** or **Seeking job**; conditional fields change without irrelevant questions.

## 4. Employer verification — 45 seconds

1. Open `/employer` and select a pending trainee.
2. Show that the employer sees the trainee/course summary needed to verify the report.
3. Confirm or correct role, joining date, wage band, and status.
4. Give a skill-relevance rating and structured feedback.
5. Save as **Verified** (or **Needs correction** to demonstrate the alternate state).

## 5. Confidence boost — 20 seconds

1. Return to `/trainee` and refresh after authentication.
2. Point out the evidence change: self-report only (60%) becomes proof-supported or employer-verified (95%).

Clarify that confidence describes source strength; it is not a guarantee of truth.

## 6. Government intelligence — 60 seconds

1. Open `/dashboard`.
2. Read the five headline metrics and their definitions.
3. Select **Gadchiroli** on the district visualization, then **Pune**, and compare local outcomes and risk drivers.
4. Walk through the enrollment → completion → placement → 6-month retention funnel.
5. Show a supply/demand sector gap such as Docker or AWS.
6. Open an attrition reason and a policy-insight card.
7. Point to the evidence attached to each insight.

The key point is traceability: every recommendation points back to a visible aggregate or structured signal.

## 7. Trust and governance close — 30 seconds

Highlight:

- consent-driven employer disclosure;
- role-scoped access;
- outcome provenance and audit logs;
- multilingual, mobile-first collection;
- no invented values in policy summaries.

For production, explain the planned controls: encryption, malware scanning, retention/deletion, MFA for administrators, rate limiting, and independent DPDP compliance review.
